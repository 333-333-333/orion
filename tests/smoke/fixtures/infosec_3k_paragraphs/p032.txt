Change management controls modifications to systems, applications, infrastructure, and configurations. A change request proposes a modification. A standard change is a type of pre-approved low-risk change. A normal change is a type of change that requires assessment and approval. An emergency change is a type of change implemented rapidly to resolve urgent issues. Change approval verifies that a change is acceptable. Change testing verifies that a change works as intended. Change rollback restores a previous state if a change fails. Unauthorized change is a type of control violation. Poor change management can introduce vulnerabilities.
