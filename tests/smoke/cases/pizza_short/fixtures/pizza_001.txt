At Mario's Pizzeria in Harbor Market, chef Sofia Rossi prepares the Saturday lunch service for three neighborhood tables. The centerpiece is a Margherita pizza called the Vesuvio pie. Sofia stretches dough made from Caputo flour, warm water, sea salt, and a slow sourdough starter. Her assistant Luca Ferretti checks that the dough was mixed on Friday morning, rested in the blue proofing box, and divided into four round balls before service.

The sauce for the Vesuvio pie comes from San Marzano tomatoes delivered by Green Valley Farm. Sofia crushes the tomatoes with basil, olive oil, and a pinch of oregano. The mozzarella is supplied by River Dairy, and each slice is marked on the prep board as fresh fior di latte. Luca grates aged parmesan over the edge, while Sofia places basil leaves in a triangle so guests can see the recipe pattern clearly.

Luigi Bianchi manages delivery orders for Mario's Pizzeria. At noon he receives an order from Central Cafe for one Vesuvio pie, one Marinara pizza, and a small arugula salad. The order ticket connects Central Cafe to table host Nina Patel, who requested extra basil and no chili flakes. Luigi confirms the order with Nina by phone, writes the destination as Central Cafe on Pine Street, and attaches the ticket to a red thermal bag.

Inside the kitchen, the wood oven named Dante burns oak logs and holds a temperature near 430 degrees Celsius. Sofia slides the Vesuvio pie onto the oven floor with a long metal peel. The crust blisters first, the tomato sauce darkens near the rim, and the mozzarella melts into white pools. Luca turns the pizza twice and records the bake time as ninety seconds in the service notebook.

When the pizza leaves the oven, Sofia adds more basil and one spoon of olive oil from Olive Grove Cooperative. She cuts the pie into six slices and places it in a box labeled order CAFE-17. Luigi scans the label, places the box beside the salad, and leaves Mario's Pizzeria on a blue bicycle. He rides from Harbor Market to Pine Street, passes the public library, and reaches Central Cafe at 12:18 p.m.

At Central Cafe, Nina Patel receives the Vesuvio pie and signs the delivery tablet. She serves two slices to barista Omar Chen, one slice to manager Elena Ruiz, and keeps three slices for the front counter staff. Nina tells Luigi that the basil aroma matches the request, and Luigi records the delivery as completed. The tablet links Mario's Pizzeria, Luigi Bianchi, Central Cafe, Nina Patel, order CAFE-17, and the Vesuvio pie in the same delivery record.

After lunch, Sofia reviews the notebook. It states that Green Valley Farm supplied tomatoes, River Dairy supplied mozzarella, Olive Grove Cooperative supplied olive oil, and Dante baked the Vesuvio pie. Sofia stores the proofing box, cleans the metal peel, and updates the recipe card for the next Saturday service.
